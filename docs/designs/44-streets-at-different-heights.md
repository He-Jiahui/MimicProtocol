# Streets at Different Heights

Status: District scene proposals. Updated: 2026-11-27. Requirements: R-01, R-02, R-03, R-10, R-13.

[Back to index](README.md) | Extends [World Structure and Buildings](09-world-structure-and-buildings.md), [City Terrain](29-presentation-creatures-and-city-terrain.md), [Upper Floors and the Way Down](32-upper-floors-and-the-way-down.md) and [Meridian Map Families and Crossings](40-meridian-map-families-and-crossings.md).

Use Chongqing as a feeling reference: the surprise of leaving a building at street level after you distinctly remember climbing to get there. Meridian's grades, streets and buildings are its own. Imagine looking *across* at someone waiting for a bus and *down* at a truck passing below the parapet at their feet. The interest is in how you could get to either person. Give a hillside a retaining face, a stair and level places to stand. A slope running through somebody's kitchen would undo the illusion. Every scene below uses the height model in [29](29-presentation-creatures-and-city-terrain.md): levels sit side by side and meet at walls, stairs and ramps, because a grid cell holds only one walkable floor, so where the original idea stacked one walkable place over another it has been moved beside it or made scenery.

**Urban core: the market that holds up a bus station.** A wholesale market survived when the city built a terminal on the terrace behind it; the market's back wall now retains the bus deck a level above. Slender residential towers rise from the bus deck; the hotel occupies a deeper wing where its kitchens can share the market's loading court. From a distance the staggered towers and a high glazed bridge read as one enormous mixed-use complex. Up close it has distinct addresses. A visitor steps off a bus into the hotel lobby. A porter arriving in the parking garage cut into the slope at market level passes the backs of the same refrigerated cabinets the cook opens from the other side.

A multi-lane intersection spreads out under the viaduct. Turning buses climb to the deck, through traffic passes overhead, and a protected pedestrian passage reaches the public stair without crossing the parking ramp. Follow that stair past the bus platforms to the hotel, climb to its linen exchange and walk the high bridge into the housing tower. The bridge spans the bus lanes, which are fenced off beneath it, so no one walks under it. People once sent bedding across to the laundry there. Looking back through the bridge, you can see the bus shelter roof but no longer read its lettering. The other tower's stair comes down beside the ticket plaza. Its wide middle once held commuters changing lines; today it gives an operator clean ground for a fight and an uninterrupted view of the street toward pickup. If the bridge looks exposed, the garage offers a covered crossing below, at the cost of losing that view until the ticket stair.

**Residential: the lane behind the good front room.** Below the villa terrace, families extended their houses toward one another a room at a time. Kitchens back onto a narrow lane so deliveries needn't pass through front rooms. Enclosed balconies project over the kitchens, their drains spliced into older downpipes. A bookseller beneath one addition wheels the display indoors every night to leave enough space for a neighbor's chair to pass. This urban village has owners, trades and private thresholds; its density comes from people making room, not from putting refuse on every path.

Follow a bread delivery up the stepped lane and it opens into a school court level with the retaining wall's top. The gaps between buildings frame the handrail of the lower street. A covered gallery along the flats reaches the gate on the upper villa road; the public zigzag stair returns to the snack street below. A municipal passage along the foot of the court's retaining wall leads past the boiler rooms to the pickup lane. Evacuees with a trolley would take the longer ramp beside the passage. In a fight the empty court lets the operator see a rush coming; staying along the gallery keeps a roof overhead but puts a blind corner between the operator and the gate. A faded school mural visible from both approaches is a better bearing than a forest of route arrows.

**Harbor: follow the catch uphill.** Boats unload at a tidal landing. A hinged gangway reaches a fixed auction floor, while an ice plant cut into the bank delivers crushed ice through chutes from a higher service road. Refrigerated lorries cross a rail cutting on an overpass to reach the back of the plant. The doors on the water side are for fish and people; the tall doors uphill are for trucks. Beyond the tank roofs the lighthouse gives the harbor a bearing even when ships hide the waterline. The empty freight apron in [40](40-meridian-map-families-and-crossings.md) can stay open because the freight congestion belongs here at the exchange.

A seawall stair climbs from auction floor to plant gallery. From the grated walk a gap between ducts frames the launch steps; a stair at the far end comes down behind the weigh house. At the foot of the seawall, a covered sorting aisle leads to the same steps, but you can't see the launch past the moored hull. A third possibility opens across the auction apron. Carts once needed that clear floor to turn a catch without spilling it. Now the operator can cross quickly in view of the quay or hold ground when the aisle is overrun. A bag worth carrying makes the view from the gallery more valuable on the return trip than it was on the way in.

Each scene is sized to one or two lots of a 500 m map, roughly 50 to 120 m across, and built from primitives by layout code, as `placeLandmarks` in `src/sim/world.js` builds the Shelter and the lair. For an authored map, fix the relationships a local would know: the school is above the boiler rooms; the auction is below the ice plant. Vary which approaches are occupied or accessible under [the map-family proposal](40-meridian-map-families-and-crossings.md). A shuttered gallery can change the return journey, while the public stair still goes where its landing promised. A crossing into a harder district is a choice within the raid. Each family deserves an ordinary way to earn extraction without crossing or awakening a boss.
