# Inheriting a Kit, Changing a Raid

Status: Comparative design research and proposals. Updated: 2026-11-27. Requirements: R-01, R-04, R-05, D-07, D-21, D-22.

[Back to index](README.md) | [Inherited roster](05-classes-and-skills.md) | [Demo kits](18-demo-classes-elites-and-items.md) | [Other games' resource decisions](50-what-a-skill-asks-of-the-player.md)

Leave a Summoner at a relay with a sentry covering the exit. Now ask them to carry the relay's recordings across the street. **StormBound helps explain why abandoning that firing position should cost something. Risk of Rain 2 helps explain how a find might make abandoning it worthwhile.** The player should recognize the same operator on both sides of the street, with or without the find.

### What to bring across from StormBound

The November 26 **StormBound skill-mechanism RESULT_CARD** reports on revision `9d02fe4`. Its sections 3, 7 and 8 distinguish the native four slots from equipped alternatives, carried reserves from claims on living targets, and ground resources from effects already spent. A replacement can change a native action's shape or rhythm; a link between slots can make one action prepare another. The base actions are available before choosing a branch. The card also distinguishes adopted browser decisions from earlier proposals and archived demonstrations. Neither its historical tuning nor the separate untracked `new_project_docs` draft establishes DarkSignal behavior.

Here is the full [05](05-classes-and-skills.md) roster in its original order. The middle column paraphrases decisions in the card's class audit, sometimes more specific than 05's earlier synopsis. The last column is **Proposal**: a question that could survive translation to military equipment. These are working names for the longer-term roster, not twenty new kits or a rollout order.

| Working name in 05 | What the StormBound player manages | Decision to keep in Meridian |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Rogue | Successive hits prepare a series of cuts; each victim holds its own accumulated finisher opportunity. | Commit to one exposed hostile or disengage and forfeit the setup on that body. Don't award it for firing into a crowd. |
| Multi-Tool Specialist | Two tools draw from one nail supply; a saw swing can miss without spending its on-contact payload. | Use recovered fasteners for close contact or for a charge at the next door. Changing tools doesn't refill the belt. |

| Artificer | Actions produce orbs; the ultimate claims those already present, leaving new arrivals for later. | Recall deployed cells for the current pulse or set up another first. The pulse mustn't refill itself. |

| Captain | A designation follows a living target, but committed fire can be locked to ground; the two beacon functions need not match. | Mark the moving contact or commit support to the crossing it must use. A fleeing contact can leave a strike behind. |

| False Son | Spikes held back provide defense; throwing them gives up that protection to expose an enemy. | Retain detachable plating on the suit or commit it to opening the brute. An attack changes the protection still on hand. |
| Chef | Returning tools and movement lay oil; further actions can transform the surface they actually reach. | Prepare a route through a service lane, then decide whether to activate the patch or leave it for the retreat. No kitchen fiction required. |

Several effects compete for a single corpse; one form can pay from health when there is none. | Use recovered hostile biomass to reinforce armor or power a field device. Provide a costly alternative against a lone living enemy; never treat civilians as supplies. |

**A variant worth studying closely:** the card distinguishes the Rogue's ordinary finisher over marked living targets from a duel variant. In the duel, damage stored on the chosen target can leave it alive for the operator's own finishing hit; the reset is tied to that real kill. The value is the *change of commitment*: the player sets up one opponent and must finish the job personally. DarkSignal needn't inherit a sliver-of-health exception or a kill reset. Blade's equivalent choice could be to spend an opening to continue through one living brute instead of clearing space around several. A kill-dependent version would give Blade the least to do in a lone boss fight.

Two rows that sound alike until someone moves are **Engineer and Operator**: one abandons a prepared position; the other gives up either a repairable drone or its salvage. Keep that difference before designing different gun models. Keep the card's choice between native variants and links across slots, too. Leave its node counts, point currencies, fantasy identities, automatic basic targeting and wave-clearing incentives behind. None explains a search, a mutually exposing scan, or why an operator might leave a fight unfinished.

### What Risk of Rain 2 lets an item say

The [base game's description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/632360/Risk_of_Rain_2/?l=english) separates a survivor's own combat style and alternate skills from the items that combine during a run. The [Survivors of the Void description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1607890/Risk_of_Rain_2_Survivors_of_the_Void/?l=english) makes the distinction visible. Railgunner looks for an overlook, a weak point and a timed reload. Void Fiend times a change in how its abilities behave. Those are different rhythms before a particular item drops. A void ukulele, by contrast, changes familiar crowd coverage into focused attacks on one enemy. It changes what a run offers the survivor; it doesn't turn the survivor into Railgunner.

| Checked item or skill | What the player can tell before taking it | DarkSignal proposal and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engineer's [Gauss turret](https://minmax.wiki/risk-of-rain-2/tr-12-gauss-auto-turret) and [mobile Carbonizer](https://minmax.wiki/risk-of-rain-2/tr-58-carbonizer-turret) both inherit items; the mobile alternative brings a slowing beam. | The choice is where the gun can be when the player moves. Changing the turret also changes how an existing item build plays. | Route the Summoner's next Mark order through a sentry **or** through escort drones. Give the first a firing lane worth holding and the second a reason to cross. Don't copy every operator trigger to every device: one command would then claim the same mark several times. |
| Ordinary [Bustling Fungus](https://minmax.wiki/risk-of-rain-2/bustling-fungus) creates a healing zone after standing still; copies improve the heal and coverage. | The first copy gives stillness a purpose. Later copies improve what already happens in that place. | A find could let a sentry hold a pending order while the operator searches a counter in its lane. A repeat could give more time to take the shot. Don't pay indefinitely for idling in an empty shop: an aimed order and a revealed target still matter. |
| Lunar [Shaped Glass](https://minmax.wiki/risk-of-rain-2/shaped-glass) raises base damage at the cost of maximum health. | A benefit and its price coexist. The same offer looks different when the player has something to lose. | Put both sides of an openly offered field modification in one sentence: a relay can carry a sentry order farther, but its transmission makes noise. Skip the health-for-damage ratio; alerting a nearby pack is a cost the player can judge from the street. |
| Void [Weeping Fungus](https://minmax.wiki/risk-of-rain-2/weeping-fungus) heals while sprinting and corrupts Bustling Fungus. The expansion page describes conversion of the normal counterparts. | The earlier stillness benefit is replaced. This is a different action to pursue, not simply another bonus or a lunar-style penalty. | An offered conversion could move a held order off the abandoned sentry onto an escort drone. State what stops working at the relay; let the player decline. Don't silently rewrite carried gear or use *void corruption* as another name for [08's contamination](08-loot-contamination-and-legendary-gear.md). |

These are examples of how to describe a choice, not four proposed item tiers. A found relay may change how long or where an order is useful. If a Barracks variant already sends the order through the drones, the same find has nothing left to convert; don't offer it as a second free shot. If another item repeats a burst, [24's](24-skill-resource-and-synergies.md) restriction on copies and [30's](30-staged-damage-calculation.md) rule about each recipient's own defense still make sense. An item that turns every tick into a fresh paid attack would drown out the aimed order. Keep [18's raid items](18-demo-classes-elites-and-items.md) distinct from [23's gear brought back for appraisal](23-demo-boss-loot-and-drops.md); see [06](06-run-growth-roguelite.md) and [07](07-meta-progression-and-difficulty.md) for the existing progression proposals.

### What the four demo operators should still be able to do with an empty bag

[18](18-demo-classes-elites-and-items.md) maps the demo to four inherited rows. Rifleman draws on Assault Trooper; Blade on Mercenary; Summoner on Engineer; Toxic Handler retains its working name. “Gunner” here means Rifleman. Today's Rifleman spends **Suppression** on Piercing Line and can ricochet; the literal **Mark** that a sentry or drone spends for splash belongs to Summoner. The gunner's chosen-target area burst below is the direction in [28](28-class-resource-loops.md), not a claim that the two demo actions are identical.

| Operator | Ordinary signature to preserve or make reachable (Proposal) | When a [Barracks alternative](39-class-levels-and-barracks-variants.md) would earn its place |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rifleman | Paint a revealed Mimic with aimed bursts. Spend on that target to catch its neighbors while they're still bunched. | At the end of a passage, roll across for a lengthwise discharge. Lose the side splash that would have helped in the waiting room. |
| Blade | Expose a living opponent with a cut and turn that opening into a useful whirl or a fragment. It works even if nothing dies and no cooldown needs a refund. | Collect on the way out of the whirl for a guarded withdrawal; going straight in no longer buys the same cover. |
| Summoner | Mark a chosen hostile and let the placed sentry answer. A first order works without previously scavenged parts. | Take the order across the street with the drone flight. Now the old sentry cannot also spend it at the relay. |
| Toxic Handler | Spit, then rupture with a cloud before the ordinary host dies. A longer cultivation is optional. | Let that infection persist through a pool and follow the pursuit. Give up the immediate clear; earlier damage doesn't become payable a second time. |

A useful paper comparison for the next design pass is to put the *same* operator in the relay doorway before and after one choice. Write down who they aim at, where they stand, and what they leave behind on extraction. If the answer is unchanged apart from a bigger hit, keep the original kit and reconsider the variant. If the base kit needs the find to answer at all, fix the base kit first. No class, unlock or pickup should be the price of getting out alive.
