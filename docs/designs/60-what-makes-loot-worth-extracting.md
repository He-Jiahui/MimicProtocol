# What Makes Loot Worth Extracting?

Status: Reference study and design proposal. Updated: 2026-11-27. Requirements: R-01, R-05, R-12, D-20.

Extends [02: Raid structure](02-core-loop-and-raid-structure.md), [23: Boss drops](23-demo-boss-loot-and-drops.md) and [38: Field bags](38-field-bags-and-stash-appraisal.md). Decisions: [61: Packing](61-packing-for-the-walk-out.md), [62: Recovery](62-what-the-insurance-drone-brings-home.md), [63: Equipment](63-what-the-operator-wears.md).

A dispatch recorder in a ferry office can do three jobs without granting a single point of damage. It gives the operator a reason to search that office, a reason to hold the doorway while packing, and a reason to pass the next unopened locker. If its sale price is banked the instant it is found, only the first of those decisions survives.

## When does the prize become mine?

**Hunt: Showdown and Dark and Darker.** [Crytek's Bounty Hunt guide](https://www.huntshowdown.com/player-guide/en_US) distinguishes taking a bounty token from extracting it for payment. [IRONMACE's description](https://www.ironmace.com/) gives finding a way out its own place after taking the treasure. Borrow Hunt's separation of victory beats: the boss falls, its sealed equipment lands, and the operator still has to bring it home. Borrow Dark and Darker's attention to the *way* out: spot the ferry gate on the outward walk, then decide whether the return crossing is worth another stop. Meridian already has Hosts, mutual sensing and a night clock. It needs neither rival human teams hunting a token nor hidden exits as a condition of earning ordinary loot.

**Escape from Duckov and the WATER.html precedent.** [Team Soda's description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3167020/Escape_From_Duckov/) connects scavenging to a use back home and warns about failing to extract in time. Give a find a destination the player can name: a recorder for the Quartermaster, a sealed piece for the Lab. Don't import a hideout crafting tree to supply that motivation. DarkSignal's [reference notes](README.md) and [02, sections 4–6](02-core-loop-and-raid-structure.md) record WATER.html's held extraction and broker trade. Keep the exposed hold. A broker can lighten the load by buying the recorder, but the proceeds must still leave the district; instant deposits would make the broker a second exit. Today's broker is exactly that: `Raid.completeInteract` in `src/sim/raid.js` pays 80% into `banked` Cash that survives death, so [62](62-what-the-insurance-drone-brings-home.md) turns the proceeds into at-risk raid Credits. WATER.html here is a project design precedent, not a fresh inspection of the prototype.

## Will it fit, and can I get it there?

**Diablo II and Resident Evil 4.** Blizzard's [Arreat Summit: Charms](https://classic.battle.net/diablo2exp/items/charms.shtml) describes differently sized charms competing for the room needed for a future large pickup. In DarkSignal, leaving one corner empty can be a plan. Leave Diablo's active backpack bonuses behind: a sealed piece is freight. Capcom's [Resident Evil 4 PS2 manual, p. 14](https://www.gamesdatabase.org/Media/SYSTEM/Sony_Playstation_2/Manual/formated/Resident_Evil_4_-_2005_-_Capcom.pdf) shows how moving and rotating a piece can make a pickup possible, with temporary space for rearrangement. Borrow the readable fit. Don't let an item waiting outside the grid travel with the operator: once they leave the office, what didn't fit stays on the floor.

**S.T.A.L.K.E.R.: Shadow of Chernobyl.** The [published manual, Inventory and Weapon Condition](https://store.steampowered.com/manual/4500) separates object size from carried weight. A heavy load tires the character sooner; enough weight can stop movement. It also describes weapon wear and increasingly frequent jams. Give the player two different answers: “the coupler fits” and “the crossing will take longer.” Leave them able to walk or drop it. Borrow visible condition across uses, but skip random failures on aimed basics: the Armory is a better place to decide whether a worn piece can make another trip.

## What is recovery buying?

**Escape from Tarkov and Arena Breakout.** [Battlestate's update 1.1.0.0 preview](https://t.me/escapefromtarkovEN/6687) describes PvP and PvE Zone insurance services that trade cost against return time and separately discusses Hideout requirements for items with Found in Raid status. Pay for a known recovery service before deployment; don't copy the wait for a trader or add a provenance qualification to Lab appraisal. “Found equipment” here simply means a loose piece picked up in the district. Zhang and colleagues' [Arena Breakout study, section II.A](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.04936) describes a secure box whose contents survive death while goods outside it are lost. Borrow the legible boundary around protected cargo. Change its timing: buy a drone before leaving, then decide what it lifts **after death**, with the whole failed haul in view. No free, permanently protected pocket. Neither reference sets DarkSignal's drone dimensions, credit allowance or loss rules.

## What was inside the seal?

Blizzard's [Diablo II Basics, Scrolls](https://classic.battle.net/diablo2exp/basics/) makes identification the moment a magic item's properties become legible and it can be used. Keep that reveal; leave out field identification scrolls. If the ferry find could be opened and worn at the gate, the best way to learn what it does would no longer be to get out alive.

**A rule can belong to a slot or to a particular piece.** Blizzard's [Diablo IV 1.2.0 item notes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24092662/diablo-iv-patch-notes-1-0-1-2) give a mobility Aspect a limited choice of slots; they also describe a Unique amulet that changes a spell's path under a particular condition. The [Season 4 Codex notes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24077223/galvanize-your-legend-in-season-4-loot-reborn) describe salvaging Legendary gear to keep reusable Aspects. Take compatibility and a rule you can picture in action. Decline unlimited imprinting: consuming a donor for one compatible receiver keeps another recovered piece worth inspecting. Grinding Gear Games' [The Awakening: New Unique Items](https://www.pathofexile.com/theawakening/items) states a design aim of opening builds through distinctive items. Take the idea of a piece worth wearing at equal rating; avoid making a rare drop the admission ticket to a class's basic loop. [63](63-what-the-operator-wears.md) separates a fixed signature from a transferable core. Neither is learned from a raid-only pickup.
