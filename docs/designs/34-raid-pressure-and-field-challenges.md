# Raid Pressure and Field Challenges

Status: Design proposal. Extends [Raid Time and Zones](17-demo-raid-map-time-and-zones.md), [In-Raid Events](20-demo-in-raid-events.md) and [Contracts and Sector States](21-demo-contracts-and-sector-states.md). [Back to index](README.md)

John's observation is that items make the operator powerful quickly, while the raid takes too long to answer. Let elapsed time bring harder encounters forward sooner. Keep an early find interesting by giving it a use, such as keeping a pursuing pack out of a stairwell. More copies of a damage item at every nearby stop would let the operator outgrow the neighborhood before making a route choice. Information, useful transport and access to an exit can all pay for a detour without accelerating combat power.

**An exceptional clear brings the later raid closer.** A high-star finish in a reward activity or challenge is evidence that the present opposition has been mastered. Let that success advance the same difficulty that normally comes with staying out longer. Approaching the next intersection, the operator might hear Command call the heavier patrol forward and recognize its carrier turning into the street. The prize stays earned and useful. An ordinary search isn't a challenge clear, and advancing opposition need not skip the sky to midnight, move curfew, redraw danger regions or raise the persistent Threat Tier. The player should know about that consequence before taking the challenge.

**Give the job a place and a beneficiary.** The official StormBound RESULT_CARD discusses rescue and seal events in *Triggers, aim and timelines*, and distinguishes older ratings and shrine restrictions from later event-specific rewards in *Interactions with items, units, bosses and modes*. The families are useful references. The following jobs and their effect on raid pressure are Meridian proposals:

- **Escort civilians.** A clinic needs to move its patients before the awning lights go out. A Rifleman can spend Suppression to clear the crossing; a Summoner can cover the rear while the patients move. Reaching the public shelter with the group intact could earn an introduction to its driver. The choice is which crossing to protect, not how many enemies to farm on it.
- **Bring a vehicle out.** A service van can evacuate the block, but its driver won't leave with the ramp blocked. Clearing the ramp and recovering a dispatch radio might secure a seat. The van's departure can be the extraction itself, so the work and the reward tell the same story.
- **Deploy key equipment.** A field relay belongs on the open quay, where it can hear the harbor launch; the raid has one walkable street level, so a roof is out of reach. Placing it in an exposed position makes the broadcast possible; staying to protect it could also give Command a bearing on a nest. The immediate call is useful this raid, while [sector contracts](21-demo-contracts-and-sector-states.md) still credit lasting work on return. Blade can dislodge a blocker; the Handler can keep the landing clear. None of the jobs requires a particular kit or a new skill slot.

**A marked exit is a destination, not permission to leave.** Refine the earlier open-sector extraction: sector access gets the operator to the quay; a working relay gets a boat to answer. Let the player learn the condition while there is still time to act and find a reachable alternative if a rescue fails. A straightforward pickup can take modest local work; the operator needn't win a boss fight or earn top marks to leave. The [boss exfil](23-demo-boss-loot-and-drops.md) that opens after the B3 kill has already been earned through that encounter; [opening an ordinary lair](35-opening-the-lair-and-fighting-the-brood.md) opens no exit. [Map crossings](40-meridian-map-families-and-crossings.md) continue the raid instead of banking loot.
