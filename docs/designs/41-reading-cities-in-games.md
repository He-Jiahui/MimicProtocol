# Reading Cities in Games

Status: Reference research and design proposal. Updated: 2026-11-27. Requirements: R-01, R-02, R-03, R-08.

[Back to index](README.md) | Extends [Vision and Pillars](00-vision-and-pillars.md), [World Structure](09-world-structure-and-buildings.md) and [Meridian Map Families](40-meridian-map-families-and-crossings.md).

What makes a place worth learning on the way in, when the operator will have to find a way out carrying something? These notes draw on public accounts by the people who made or published the games. The Meridian examples are our proposals, not descriptions of those games or impressions from a playtest.

**Ghostwire: Tokyo — different ages of light on one street.** In [Bethesda's conversation with director Kenji Kimura](https://bethesda.net/en-US/news/ghostwire-tokyo-gameplay-reveal-and-next-gen-details), a brightly lit modern building shares a city with an old bathhouse tucked between newer neighbors. The [PlayStation Blog trailer article](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=353150) calls attention to the rain on the streets. In Meridian, imagine a tower lobby still spilling white light through the old cobbler's wired-glass transom after the shop has shut. From across the wet road you can distinguish a lit room from a closed door. Keep Ghostwire as a lighting and atmosphere reference; its crossings, ghosts and emptied streets belong to a different story. Meridian needs people you hesitate to shoot.

**Dishonored 2 — a city in section.** Bethesda's [Creating Karnaca](https://bethesda.net/en-EU/news/dishonored-2-creating-karnaca) connects quarrying to the shape of districts and architectural study to routes at different heights. In [Christophe Carrier's account of the Clockwork Mansion](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/level-design-deep-dive-i-dishonored-2-s-i-clockwork-mansion), service passages give views back into principal rooms; the recognizable use of a room helps the player keep their bearings. Imagine a Meridian resident saying, “Come in by the pool; the other front door is up on the bus road.” From the basement laundry you can see wet feet pass the pool window and know which stair you want. No transforming mansion or quarry city is needed: a hillside and a history of municipal additions explain why the routes meet.

**ARC Raiders — an open space changes meaning on the return.** Embark's [Riven Tides scout report](https://arcraiders.com/news/riven-tides-map-scout-report) describes the beach as exposed low ground and the dockyard as a place with height, hiding places and cover. It is a fictional scout's account, not evidence about encounter balance. In Meridian the fish market shutters make good shelter on the outward search. Coming home, those same shutters hide the boat; the auction apron you avoided earlier is where you can see whether the launch has come alongside. Leave Embark's resort, coastal itinerary and rival Raiders behind. The risk here is crossing with an open Resonance Link and a catch you have yet to extract.

**Little Nightmares II — what a ward promises.** Bandai Namco's [character notes](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/little-nightmares/little-nightmares-ii) give the Patients a wish to be fixed and the Doctor a fixation on perfection. Its [presentation notes](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/news/dive-unforgettable-nightmare-little-nightmares-ii-enhanced-edition) pick out the ward tiles and the Doctor working by a swinging lamp. Read together, they suggest why ordinary signs of care can become frightening. For Meridian, a soldier's own handwriting on a discharge request is more disturbing than a wall of specimen jars: someone was well enough to ask to leave. The military trial is our fiction. Keep the Doctor, Patients and their particular horror out of it so the soldier can remain a person with a history.

**Scorn — a building with an appetite.** In [Ebb Software's account on Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2022/09/29/scorns-nightmarish-hellscape-comes-to-xbox-series-xs-a-week-early/), industrial and Gothic forms share an environment informed by biology; the team describes objects whose purposes the visitor can infer from their surroundings. In Meridian, rain disappears into an ordinary storm inlet and the pavement farther uphill swallows. You know enough about street drainage to notice which way the meal went. Leave Scorn's sculpted forms and puzzles in its world. The horror of [the Descent Ward](42-meridian-after-the-descent.md) depends on recognizing a public utility that now serves something else.
