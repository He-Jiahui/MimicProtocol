# What the Insurance Drone Brings Home

Status: Design direction with proposed settlement examples. Updated: 2026-11-27. Requirements: R-01, R-05, R-12.

Revises [02: Death and loss](02-core-loop-and-raid-structure.md), [06: Raid Credits](06-run-growth-roguelite.md), [07: Hub upgrades](07-meta-progression-and-difficulty.md) and [08: Insurance](08-loot-contamination-and-legendary-gear.md). Extends [38: Appraisal](38-field-bags-and-stash-appraisal.md) and clarifies [57: What crosses extraction?](57-what-crosses-extraction.md).

**Leaving alive is the win. The Insurance Drone buys one decision after a loss.** At the Quartermaster, buy a drone and assign it to the next deployment. The purchase tells you two things: the shape of its cargo bay and the maximum raid Credits it can recover. Alternatively, find a sealed drone in the district, bring it home as cargo and have the Lab appraise it. It can be assigned on a *later* deployment. Finding one in the ferry office cannot insure the current trip. One assigned drone, no advance tagging of valuables, no stacking coverage. It is a consumable, separate from both the worn kit and the field pack.

## Two endings at the ferry gate

**Extract alive.** The operator brings home the pack and all its remaining cargo. The sealed parcel goes to the stash still sealed. Settle eligible contracts, extraction rewards and the ordinary conversion of unspent raid Credits just once. The worn kit returns in its used condition. Proposal: an assigned drone that did not fly returns available for another raid. Nothing is in the hub merely because it was picked up or sold in the street.

**Die before the hold finishes.** Freeze the contents that were actually being carried. At debrief let the player select whole eligible pieces from that cargo and see the remainder marked lost before confirming. No fetching the coupler left at the booth. A packed object must fit inside the drone in its own right; a container cannot rescue nested goods. The active backpack drops and cannot rescue itself. The assigned drone is spent or lost even if the player recovers nothing. Without an assigned drone, no carried items or raid Credits come back. Curfew or a raid ending as a death uses the same rule.

**Proposal example.** A 2-by-2 drone bay can lift the 2-by-2 sealed parcel, **or** a 1-by-2 medical sample and two 1-by-1 components. It cannot lift a rigid 1-by-3 recorder, even empty. Let the player choose the parcel with only its quality showing; don't rank the choices by their hidden rolls. A fitting Tainted parcel can be recovered still Tainted: rescue neither identifies nor cleans it. Alternatively, saving the samples might let the Lab open gear already waiting at home. This is a choice about what the operator needs, not an automatic award to the two most expensive objects.

**Credits travel separately.** Proposal arithmetic: with 90 unspent raid Credits and a drone allowance of 30, the player may return up to 30; at least 60 are lost. With only 12 on hand the maximum is 12. The allowance costs no item cells, gives no credit advance and never touches Cash already held at the hub. Settle just the recovered amount through the ordinary raid-Credits-to-hub-Cash conversion once; the proposed percentage in [06](06-run-growth-roguelite.md) is a separate economy decision. (The build at `81870a5` converts no unspent Credits at all, so this conversion has to be added before a drone allowance means anything.) A street broker can exchange cargo for spendable raid Credits, but those proceeds join this same at-risk balance. No death bonus, contract payout, boss payout or extra insured wallet. Class XP and previously committed progression keep their own rules; neither counts as recovered loot.

At debrief the player should be able to say: “I kept my deployed kit, I spent my drone to save the parcel and some Credits, and I lost the pack and the recorder.” A [59 run effect](59-what-a-raid-item-can-do.md) ends even if it helped defend the gate. It is never a cargo candidate. The [worn kit](63-what-the-operator-wears.md) needs no drone cell and keeps any wear incurred. The failure still matters.

## Which older promises change?

- [02 sections 2, 5, 7 and 8](02-core-loop-and-raid-structure.md): replace the Quartermaster's “insure items” service with selling deployment drones. Replace “Lost unless insured” for equipped gear, the guaranteed insured/case rows, the immediately banked broker proceeds, the fixed surviving grid and check 02-A-02. The old carried-loot loss row now has the drone exception. Keep the extraction hold, interruption and extraction-only contract payout.
- [08 sections 4, 6 and 8](08-loot-contamination-and-legendary-gear.md): retire per-item premiums, automatic returns, expandable insurance slots, the two-item demo case and the broker's immediate deposit. “Cannot be insured” on Tainted goods no longer bars a lift; the Lab still handles contamination. [07 section 1](07-meta-progression-and-difficulty.md) should not sell permanent insurance slots or a larger immortal case. If hub progress widens drone stock, each deployed drone is still at risk.
- [06 sections 2 and 6](06-run-growth-roguelite.md): worn gear no longer risks confiscation; the blanket loss of unspent Credits has one bounded exception. [15's secure-case override](15-web-demo-scope-and-overrides.md) and [19's death paragraph](19-demo-core-loop-and-progression.md) describe the older demo. The current `Raid.finish` chooses two items by value; this proposal replaces that choice, without changing the running demo. In [57](57-what-crosses-extraction.md), split “recovered gear follows the stash and loss rules” into kept worn gear versus cargo returned alive or selected by the drone. Its ownership rule stays intact.
