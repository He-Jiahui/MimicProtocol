# What a Raid Item Can Do

Status: Design proposal. Updated: 2026-11-27. Requirements: R-01, R-05, R-12, D-10, D-20, D-22.

[57: What crosses extraction?](57-what-crosses-extraction.md) | [18: Demo run items](18-demo-classes-elites-and-items.md) | [23: Boss loot](23-demo-boss-loot-and-drops.md)

A clinic cache offers a choice before the walk back to extraction. Lightning helps clear two revealed pursuers at the door. Bleed lets the operator wound the brute outside and move while it is still losing health. Either find works for Blade or Summoner: an aimed cut or a grenade striking the chosen hostile directly can start it. Neither needs a particular resource, pet or talent. The choice matters because of the route still to walk.

The [Risk of Rain 2 store description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/632360/Risk_of_Rain_2/) describes collected items combining in surprising ways over a run. Borrow that invitation to improvise. The following are **DarkSignal proposals**, not a list of that game's items or rules. Use the cache, shrine and elite sources in [06](06-run-growth-roguelite.md) and [18](18-demo-classes-elites-and-items.md); this does not require adding a level-up draft.

| Find | What changes for this raid | Decision it leaves with the player |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chain lightning | A deliberate hit can arc to another nearby, already revealed hostile with a clear path. | Choose the first target. A miss cannot find one for you. |
| Bleed | A direct wound keeps hurting that target as the operator breaks contact. | Leave a wounded pursuer behind or spend another attack to finish it now. |
| Poison | Repeated direct hits can keep a short item poison active. It supplies no class Venom and cannot be harvested for a Rupture. | Stay on the brute to maintain pressure or switch targets to protect the exit. |
| Attack speed | Shorten the interval between manual basic attacks for this raid. | More opportunities to act, each still aimed; an item's extra damage does not count as another basic. |
| Burn | A hit can leave a brief hot patch under the revealed target. | Fight at a choke to keep pursuers in it, or give up the patch to move on. |

**Stack the effect the player chose.** A second bleed find might extend the wound's remaining duration with a smaller benefit; it neither creates another independent wound on that strike nor repays elapsed ticks. A speed duplicate contributes to the same direct speed bonus with diminishing benefit. It does not improve a persistent attribute or separately accelerate clouds, drones and recovery timers. For on-hit items, judge the opportunity by the aimed attack: a burst landing several rounds or a grenade catching a pack does not roll separately for every body. Sentry shots, copied casts, arcs and ticks do not start a new sequence of item triggers. Extra opportunities from faster basics should still leave a slow, deliberate strike a worthwhile way to use the find.

Follow [30: Staged damage](30-staged-damage-calculation.md): give the arc its own starting strength and let its recipient's defenses answer it. Do not start from the first victim's final health loss and apply the shooter's bonuses again. Frontal plating can take much of a hit, yet a sufficiently strong hit can punch through. Follow [37: Lurkers among civilians](37-lurkers-among-civilians.md) even when a pack crosses a crowd: area effects skip everyone who looks human, including disguised lurkers. No spark, status tick or burn patch can identify one. Only a deliberate aimed finishing hit can break a disguise by damage; scanning still opens the mutual link.

**A boss can give two reasons to care about the same kill.** In [23](23-demo-boss-loot-and-drops.md), the Boss Cache offers a run effect for the remaining streets. The boss can also leave an Unidentified, Tainted piece with a latent rule. Carrying that piece grants nothing in the fight. The player can take the cache choice and still face a meaningful decision about carrying the sealed prize out. Neither reward rolls a better version of the other.

- **Persistent aspect or Power Core.** In [08's full design](08-loot-contamination-and-legendary-gear.md), bring Mimic gear home, appraise it, decontaminate it, then choose whether to consume the donor for a core and install that core in compatible equipment. The effect belongs to the fitted piece. Blizzard's [Diablo IV Season 4 notes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24077223/diablo-iv-patch-notes) describe keeping aspects from salvaged Legendaries for later imprinting. The useful idea here is deciding what gear should carry a rule; DarkSignal need not grant a reusable account unlock. The demo design uses **Process** instead: doc 24's slot Aspect and doc 23's proposed fixed Legendary Latent Power are distinct gear rules. [26](26-equipment-loot-reference-brushwild.md) distinguishes those proposals from implemented gear; core transfer is outside demo scope.
- **Persistent unique ("dark-gold").** If pursued later, bind its distinctive rule and drawback to the recovered piece itself. A unique rig might hold a sentry order through a move between positions but leave the operator unable to redirect that order in transit. Compare the rig against the displaced piece after appraisal. Its defining rule cannot be extracted into a generic core, bought as a tree node or collected as a run stack. This is a possible item identity, not a new demo rarity requirement.
- **Raid-only exception.** A rare cache could instead offer any operator a chain that returns to the first revealed target rather than seeking another. Label the choice “this raid”; it changes the path of its own discharge and works even if no coil was found earlier. It expires on death or extraction, leaves no donor or unlock, and never appears on processed equipment. Rarity tells the player how unusual the choice is. The reward description tells them what they can take home.
