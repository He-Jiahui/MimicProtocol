# Combat Feel: What to Borrow from Brushwild

Status: Draft v0.1. Updated: 2026-11-26. Requirements: R-07, D-19, D-21.

[Back to index](README.md) | Previous: [24 Skill Resource and Synergies](24-skill-resource-and-synergies.md) | Next: [26 Equipment and Loot](26-equipment-loot-reference-brushwild.md)

**Proposal:** Start with the Brute charge. A player who reads the lane and walks out should see an opening to shoot; one who rolls through contact should know why their Charge increased. These are different successes. Making them legible is more valuable to an extraction raid than slowing every shot or steering the operator toward a target.

## 1. Reference and current behavior

**Enemy warnings.** Brushwild pairs each special with a specific answer. In `src/game/systems/specials.ts`, a leap marks its landing, a cloud warns of persistent danger, a shot signals a release, a slam marks an area, and guard delays the warning until the snap. A charge fixes a rectangular lane on commit; running out of lane or hitting a wall can leave the attacker Winded. `windup` multiplies a special's time by the player setting, difficulty and an elite factor (0.85 for an elite). Basic attacks use their own timing path in `src/game/systems/ai.ts`. The longer-warnings slider spans 1–1.8; `DIFFICULTY` in `src/game/content/monsters.ts` ranges from 1.35 on Story to 0.9 on Nightmare.

DarkSignal already communicates both *where* and *when*. `tele` and `hunterBrain` in `src/sim/behave.js` give the Walker a locked cone and the Brute charge a narrow wedge; the Mote instead sights along a line and the Spitter cues its shot with sound. `src/render/scene.js` draws a fixed ground outline, a growing fill and a brighter final 22%; `src/ui/hud.js` flags off-screen wind-ups. `MELEE_TOKENS` and `SHOT_BUDGET` limit simultaneous commitments. The Brute already recovers for 2 s on wall collision versus 1.3 s at the end of an unobstructed charge. A new stun is not needed to test whether players notice this opening.

The Matron is the stronger reference for DarkSignal boss timing. Brushwild's `MonsterAI.affixes` adds a half-health enrage and summons. DarkSignal's `slam`, `sporeRain`, `chainRoar`, `toPhase` and `bossDamaged` (`src/sim/boss.js`) differentiate a locked slam, staggered ground circles, and three strikes 0.9 s apart. The 70% and 35% transitions, cysts, and poise breaks change the player's task. Cast tokens can invalidate the slam and chain; rain is scheduled separately. Preserve that distinction when changing warning lengths.

## 2. Proposal: three decisions for the demo

1. **Show what missed.** Give the Brute a constant-width neon lane derived from its locked direction, body contact width and traversable distance. Keep the sweep, hard edges and warning sound. Label the existing recovery on a missed charge (for example, **Overextended** over the Brute). Award nothing for a sidestep; show **Clean dodge** and the actual Charge gained only on the existing resource event, including zero gain at capacity. A phase change, interrupted tell or attack that was never near the operator is not a dodge. Try this encounter before adding a new enemy.
2. **Let players lengthen a tell without learning a false impact time.** Offer Normal (1.0), Longer (1.35) and Longest (1.8). Resolve the duration once when the enemy commits; use it for the actor's wind-up, the floor animation, off-screen cue and hit or release time. Adjust the boss busy time too. For Chain Roar, lengthen the lead to the first slam and retain the authored 0.9 s beat. Do not stretch a projectile's flight, a damage pool, recovery, S invulnerability or the raid clock with this setting. Keep warning times independent of `TIERS` (`src/sim/data.js`) initially, as they are today: T1–T3 already increase health, damage and elite pressure. Test tier speed separately; never silently negate Longer on T3. Offer a steady high-contrast edge in place of the final flash.
3. **Reserve time emphasis for hits a player can identify.** Compare current holds against a trial with no hold for ordinary bullets or ticks, 30 ms at 10% simulation speed for a crit, 50 ms for heavy hurt, and 80 ms for an elite kill or poise break. Use the strongest simultaneous event; limit cumulative hold time to 100 ms per real second so sustained fire cannot repeatedly stall the raid. Scale time entering the fixed-step accumulator; continue sampling input and drawing the reticle. Remember a brief S (Shift) press until the next eligible simulation step during the hold, consume it once, and discard it on pause or raid end. Do not queue repeated M1 shots. Keep shake independently adjustable and offer hit-stop Off. Sell rifle weight with a short muzzle cue, a tracer ending at actual contact, and a heavier impact cue on the beam; a visual streak must never announce a hit before the simulation does.

**Proposal: defer aim assist to a separate trial.** Keep primary aim manual and retain both camera poses. If touch trials show systematic misses, try a small, opt-in directional correction only for visible, revealed hostiles near the indicated aim line. Off means no correction; aiming into empty space or at a possible civilian remains possible. Never initiate pursuit, fire a shot, pull a skill through cover or steer a ground mark. Do not import Brushwild's generic evade, auto-attack, orbit controls or art style.

Open decision: does the lane plus recovery cue improve players' ability to describe their mistake? Test that independently of slow motion. No timing, readability or comfort result is claimed from source inspection.
