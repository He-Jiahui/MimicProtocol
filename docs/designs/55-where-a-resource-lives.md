# Where a Resource Lives

Status: Design proposal. Updated: 2026-11-27. Requirements: R-01, R-04, R-05, D-21, D-22.

[Skill Resource and Synergies](24-skill-resource-and-synergies.md) · [Class Resource Loops](28-class-resource-loops.md) · [Four Kits Beyond Charge](56-four-kits-beyond-charge.md)

John reports that Charge is too often full. Consider a Rifleman at a clinic door: the meaningful thing about to expire is the shot at the marked Mimic before it leaves the pack. More room in the Charge bank buys storage, but it cannot bring that shot back. Design the resource around what the player can still do, where they must be to do it, and what they give up by waiting.

**Proposal vocabulary.** The first three forms below describe quantity. The next two describe where something waits. The last two describe a trade. They can describe the same thing: Exposed can be both *binary* and *on an enemy*. These are alternatives for designers, not seven readouts for the player.

| Form | When it earns its place | Short DarkSignal proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Continuous value | A partial amount already does something useful; waiting a little changes the decision. | Blade builds momentum through aimed contact. A little carries a moving whirl past the nearest attack; more lets it travel farther. Break contact to search and momentum ebbs. If only “full” does anything, use a ready signal instead. |
| A handful of points | The player can count how many *commitments* remain without doing division. | Rifleman carries a few loaded cells. Spend the last one to repeat a marked shot, or keep it for the next crossing. A partial cell promises no shot. The first ordinary mark burst shouldn't need a hoard. |
| Present or absent | The question is whether an opening exists. Repeating the setup shouldn't make several copies. | A Blade cut opens a guard. While that stance is visible, whirl to break through or leave the enemy open for Execution. Opening the same guard twice still leaves one opening. |
| On an enemy | Picking a target and keeping access to it matter. | Venom goes round the corner with its host. Rupture it before losing sight, or let it keep working. The Handler doesn't inherit a reserve on the next enemy when this one dies. |
| In the room | The cost is the position or the return trip. | A loose guard fragment can be crossed once for cover; a sentry keeps a firing lane; an acid trail keeps working where the slide happened. Passing a fragment on the way to extraction should feel like giving up cover, not leaving valuable gear on the floor. |
| Reserves that compete | Gaining readiness in one place reduces what can be done elsewhere. | A Summoner can carry a field unit or commit it to a post. Deploying strengthens the lane and leaves less hardware packed for the crossing. Recovering the unit reverses that trade: the old post falls silent. An order can change what the post shoots; issuing orders cannot manufacture more units. |
| Tied to a combat attribute | Paying with an existing defense or movement option creates a risk that another meter wouldn't convey. | Cashing Blade's fragment turns position into a brief shield. A different kit might pay real health to seed a patch of ground. Being wounded on the way out then matters; an automatic full refund would erase the decision. |

**What crosses the threshold?** Picture backing out of the clinic. The loaded cell crosses with the operator, the open guard follows the creature, and the acid stays on the tiles. A spent cell may be worth remembering at the next door; a missed fragment needn't send the player back upstairs after a fight. Use the location itself as the cue: a change in the creature's stance, a cell visible on the rifle, a sentry folding up when its lane goes quiet. Keep combat remnants distinct from [gear to extract](08-loot-contamination-and-legendary-gear.md) and [boss drops](23-demo-boss-loot-and-drops.md). No part of this reserve becomes stash wealth or class XP. [Raid growth](06-run-growth-roguelite.md) ends with the outing; the [Barracks choice](39-class-levels-and-barracks-variants.md) is what persists.

**One future use: REX.** [Doc 05](05-classes-and-skills.md) lists this inherited working name with health-paid seeds and a drain garden. For a future DarkSignal version, suppose a seed costs health now and a living hostile in the resulting patch offers gradual recovery. If the hostile moves, the health is still spent. Planting while a brute holds a doorway can make sense; doing it beside an open exit with a wound and a bag of intel may be foolish. Keep a manually aimed, health-free basic and a way out. A crowded patch shouldn't repay the same expenditure in full for every body or pay for an endless chain of seeds. This is one possible use of the health trade, not a fifth demo operator or a claim about StormBound's tuning.

A useful filter for a [tree variant](39-class-levels-and-barracks-variants.md): finish the sentence “With this equipped, I'd stand **here** instead, or spend **now** instead, because…” If all that changes is the size of the reward for exactly the same action, the resource hasn't given the operator a new choice.
