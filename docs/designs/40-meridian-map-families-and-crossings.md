# Meridian Map Families and Crossings

Status: Design proposal. Extends [World Structure](09-world-structure-and-buildings.md), [Procedural Generation](10-procedural-generation.md) and [Presentation and City Terrain](29-presentation-creatures-and-city-terrain.md). [Back to index](README.md)

A familiar city needn't mean a memorized shopping route. The fixed introductory district in [Raid Map, Time of Day and Zones](17-demo-raid-map-time-and-zones.md) can teach the journey from shelter to pickup. Beyond that introduction, vary the safe insertion area, street connections, interiors and regional danger from raid to raid. Today every raid reuses `CITY_SEED` in `src/main.js` and the Shelter sits at the fixed `INSERT_X`, `INSERT_Z` in `src/sim/world.js`, so both become inputs to `generateWorld`. A depot shelter might be safe on arrival while the container yard beyond it is overrun; on a return raid the shelter might be beside the market and the dangerous route runs under the viaduct. Let the operator read those differences before committing. A new seed shouldn't invalidate the survey work on a named sector in [Contracts and Sector States](21-demo-contracts-and-sector-states.md).

**Author the character of a setting; vary the routes it supports.** Keep Meridian's rain, neon and level places to stand. Stairs, retaining walls, piers, overpasses and building platforms carry changes in elevation, using the one-floor-per-cell height model in [29](29-presentation-creatures-and-city-terrain.md). Each family is one 500 m generated map like today's district, and each draws on the live styles: the urban core on Arcology Heights and Corporate Plaza, the residential streets on Old Town Alleys and Neon Strip, the harbor on Dockside. Different settings should offer more than different facades:

- **Urban core.** Tall towers share service lobbies, loading courts and [upper-floor routes](32-upper-floors-and-the-way-down.md). A lit bridge over a rail cutting may link an archive tower to a hotel; on another visit a shutter at its end sends the operator down past the kitchens. The plaza below stays flat enough to read a charge across it. A rooftop objective on a podium no higher than 12 m makes the position of the public stairs worth remembering.
- **Residential streets.** Lower housing blocks surround a plaza and snack street. A stair down into a sunken culvert can lead to the municipal sewers (an underpass beneath a walkable road would stack two floors in one cell); a villa street on a higher terrace offers a quieter way around. Dense shrubs spill through garden railings, trees crowd an abandoned courtyard, and trampled openings show where people still walk. A civilian escort can follow a well-lit arcade or use the dry service passage a level below it. A familiar district can feel changed by which of those routes is occupied.
- **Harbor.** Beyond the map edge the river opens to the sea past a lighthouse. Moored ships interrupt sightlines along the quay, packed containers form aisles, and intricate industrial plant links pump halls, pipe galleries and service gantries. Beyond the fence the runway lights are backdrop, since an airfield would not fit a 500 m map; inside it a broad, empty freight apron offers the exposed alternative to the freight maze. The lighthouse keeps its value as a bearing even when the usable gangway, rail gate and sheltered route to the launch change.

**Crossing is a wager; extraction ends it.** An elite might guard a freight crossing into the harbor. Defeating or getting past the guard gives access to the next in-raid map with the same bag at risk. The game builds a single city at start-up today; a crossing would generate the next map off the main thread while the operator approaches, then swap it in behind a short fade carrying the clock, pressure, health and bag. Crossing advances raid difficulty: Command might warn that the freight alarm has brought the later patrols forward. The pressure persists across the boundary rather than resetting to a fresh opening or stacking an unrelated combat multiplier. The player can decline the crossing and work toward a pickup nearby.

A marked extraction point still needs local work, as in [Field Challenges](34-raid-pressure-and-field-challenges.md): reach a radio to bring the launch alongside, or clear the ramp for the [evacuation van](31-presentation-craft-and-interactables.md). A [boss offering or detector fix](35-opening-the-lair-and-fighting-the-brood.md) opens an encounter, not a general travel permit. Leave a discoverable, reachable way to earn departure without crossing an elite gate or awakening a boss. A shelter is safe ground on insertion, not an automatic cash-out. On a good seed the hardest question at the pier is whether the operator wants to leave, not whether the map happened to generate the missing key behind the exit it unlocks.
