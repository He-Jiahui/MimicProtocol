# Calculating Raid Stats and Damage

## From training to an action

For each direct stat, start with the kit or tool's base. Add the relevant aptitude contribution and other flat adjustments in the same unit. For ordinary percentage bonuses to that quantity, scale this subtotal by **one plus their sum**. The source still determines when a bonus expires; it doesn't determine how it multiplies. A repeated item may have diminishing returns under [18](18-demo-classes-elites-and-items.md): resolve its whole stack first, then enter that result once. When a condition such as “against a marked target” can't be known in the Barracks, keep the line conditional until the action qualifies.

**Proposal arithmetic — opening a cabinet.** Suppose the tool takes 5 seconds and Handling's contribution removes 1 second. A glove gives 10% faster hand searches and a raid find gives another 15%. Both describe *work rate*: the search takes `(5 − 1) / (1 + 0.10 + 0.15) = 3.2 seconds`. The shorter time is the whole benefit. It doesn't lower the chance of a Host, silence a search or waive interruption. The next deployment with the glove alone would take about 3.64 seconds regardless of how many cabinets the operator opened.

Use the unit the player is buying. Attack speed changes the interval between attacks, not damage per round. A critical-chance bonus in *percentage points* adds to the eligible hit's probability; the familiar “+5 points” takes 10% to 15%. It doesn't also change the critical hit's size. Resistance describes the share of a named incoming harm removed; acid resistance has no claim on impact damage or contamination buildup unless a separate rule says so. Move speed changes the time exposed on a crossing, not the required hold at extraction.

## From an aimed hit to health lost

1. **Establish contact and the action's base.** Name the source (round, aimed blade strike, sentry shot, poison tick) and what it can hit. Basic aim stays manual. Under [37](37-lurkers-among-civilians.md), only a deliberate aimed hit that finishes the apparent body can cause a damage reveal; it exposes a living elite. Splash, pools, chains and automatic fire leave human-looking units untouched. A rig scan remains a separate, mutual reveal.
2. **Build the outgoing amount.** Start with the action's own base and eligible flat damage. Add applicable ordinary damage bonuses in one pool, whether labeled weapon, skill, conditional, gear or raid. A line with several matching tags enters once. A preview that already includes an affix isn't a fresh base to which that affix can be added again. Apply a distinct factor only for a named event with its own consequence, such as a critical strike on an eligible hit. Ongoing harm doesn't inherit a critical hit. A Brand spend can change *where* damage lands without also multiplying the hit.
3. **Let each recipient answer.** Read its qualifying conditions, facing and relevant resistance at impact. Ordinary “takes more damage” lines on that recipient share one vulnerability bonus; the same line cannot also enter the attacker's pool. Apply the remaining damage fraction after defense, then let any barrier absorb up to its remaining strength. The rest reaches health. A facing plate gives high reduction with a nonzero remainder: a heavy shot can still kill through the front, while a flank can save that expenditure. For an incoming avoidable attack, a successful dodge prevents contact before mitigation; a clean-dodge reward isn't extra resistance.
4. **Resolve what the event earned.** For a spending hit, use the Brand recorded at contact to authorize one payoff, then remove it. Spending it does not also leave it active as a vulnerability for that hit or its discharge. If the spend buys an area discharge, use the discharge's own base or a stated share of outgoing damage before recipient defenses. In the latter case, say whether the share is taken before or after a critical strike. A share that includes the crit cannot crit again; bonuses already in that share are spent. Each neighbor answers for itself; the original victim's vulnerability, plate, barrier and lost health never become the next attack's base. A refund belongs to the Consume, not to every splash victim.

**Proposal arithmetic — a shot through the front.** On an eligible shot with base 80, a gear line of +15% damage and a raid line of +10% give `80 × (1 + 0.15 + 0.10) = 100`. Suppose this shot crits for ×1.5 and the facing plate lets one fifth through. With no other defense or barrier, 30 reaches health. An otherwise identical critical flank hit deals 150. A target with 25 health can fall to the frontal hit; the plate still mattered. These round numbers illustrate ownership and order, not target coefficients.

## Changing a mechanism without paying twice

In [28](28-class-resource-loops.md) and [39](39-class-levels-and-barracks-variants.md), the proposed Rifleman can spend Suppression for a burst among revealed enemies; a variant sends that discharge down the aimed lane *instead*. Keep the original four slots and one spend. Summoner's Mark remains a separate order to its hardware. A sentry can use its owner's eligible damage contribution when deriving the shot, without counting it again as an operator hit. A Handler converting an infection can spend only its remaining harm; remove the converted portion from future ticks.

Give an Aspect or a unique effect the same narrow entry point: name its trigger, the one quantity or action it changes, and what its follow-up may trigger. A reduced copy can use a declared share of a spender; it can't start another copy or claim another payment of the original cost. A run effect expires with the run; an item-bound rule applies only while its eligible piece is equipped. Neither can write back to an aptitude. The [demo gear comparison](26-equipment-loot-reference-brushwild.md) can describe such a rule beside the stats without inventing a permanent damage score for it.

**Earlier wording narrowed.** For this proposal, this replaces the blanket answer “With the operator's damage multipliers” for flat follow-ups in [24, section 10](24-skill-resource-and-synergies.md). A flat Rupture or Mark burst starts with its own amount and uses only bonuses eligible for that event, once. It cannot inherit the triggering hit's final damage as well.
