# What a Skill Asks of the Player

Status: Comparative design research and proposals. Updated: 2026-11-27. Requirements: R-01, R-04, R-05, D-21, D-22.

[Back to index](README.md) | [Class resource loops](28-class-resource-loops.md) | [StormBound and Risk of Rain 2](51-inheriting-kits-and-changing-the-raid.md)

A Blade operator knocks a plate loose from a living Mimic. The piece lands beyond the clinic door. Going through that door could buy protection for the next close exchange; leaving now would get the intel home. Compare that with a full Charge orb after the same cut. In one case the player has to look at the doorway. In the other they only need to check whether M2 is ready. This is a proposed scene, drawn from [28](28-class-resource-loops.md), and a useful way to judge the references below: **what would I do differently on the next input?**

### When does saving a resource become an actual choice?

Blizzard's World of Warcraft pages give two different answers. A [Warrior](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/game/classes/warrior) can gain Rage by dealing or taking damage; the strongest attacks draw on the exchange already underway. A [Rogue](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/game/classes/rogue) has both Energy and combo points; a sequence of attacks prepares a finisher. One rewards being in the fight; the other lets the player prepare a particular moment within it. Blizzard's historical [Legion Death Knight preview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/19955663/legion-class-preview-series-death-knight) explains an especially useful change: Death Strike was moved off runes so an earlier rune spend couldn't by itself lock out the heal. Discrete runes still recharged, and spending them generated Runic Power. The reason for separating two costs matters more than the number of counters.

**Proposal.** Let a Summoner who has just placed a sentry still call its first target with a deliberate grenade. If both the hardware and the first order need the same last bit of Charge, placing the sentry can buy silence at exactly the moment the player expects a reply. Borrow the protection against spending oneself out of the signature. Leave behind the MMO rotation and any requirement to absorb damage for fuel: deliberately taking a hit with a full field bag should rarely be sound play. [24](24-skill-resource-and-synergies.md) explains why hits, refunds and Overdrive all feed the current shared loop; adding a second full bar would leave [28's](28-class-resource-loops.md) pacing problem intact.

### Who owns the opening if the operator changes targets?

In [Sekiro's combat guide](https://support.activision.com/sekiro/articles/sekiro-shadows-die-twice-game-mechanics), attacks and deflections damage the opponent's Posture; lower Vitality slows its recovery. A broken stance briefly exposes that particular opponent to a Deathblow. In the [Dead Cells update that introduced Barbed Tips](https://deadcells.com/patchnotes/23), arrows lodged in a particular enemy determine the mutation's ongoing damage. Switching targets changes the answer in both games: the first opponent keeps its wound or lodged arrows. By contrast, [Path of Exile's Discharge](https://poedb.tw/us/Discharge) spends the **character's** charges; the kinds removed determine the elements of the nearby hit. What was earned against one enemy can be spent when another group arrives.

**Proposal.** At the clinic, let Blade earn an opening from ordinary aimed contact while the brute is still alive. Blade can exploit *that brute's* exposed side now, or recover the loose fragment and carry protection into a retreat. The distinction should survive a target switch: changing victims doesn't transfer a missing plate. Leave behind Sekiro's compulsory finishing sequence, Dead Cells' incentive to keep embedding ammunition for more ticking damage, and a screen-wide collection of elemental charge types. A front plate in DarkSignal reduces damage heavily; a strong enough hit still punches through and clears the horde. The plate is a reason to choose an angle, never a key needed to turn immunity off. See [25](25-combat-feel-reference-brushwild.md) and [30](30-staged-damage-calculation.md).

### Can the room remember an action?

The [Destiny 2 Behemoth reference](https://www.destinypedia.com/Behemoth) describes an Aspect, Tectonic Harvest, that rewards collecting Stasis shards with armor, and a Fragment, Whisper of Conduction, that draws nearby shards toward the player. The small change is consequential: it changes who crosses the distance. A different example is the Crown Prince in *Gunfire Reborn*. A [2021 player guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2448428883) describes Smoke Grenades applying decay and an Energy Echo ascension that gives the orb the held weapon's element. The order of two actions can change what happens to an enemy in the cloud. This is a dated interaction, not a claim about current rankings.

**Proposal.** Let a Blade withdrawal collect a fragment on the lunge's actual path. A fragment on the other side of a wall stays there; permanent pickup attraction would erase the route decision. For Toxic Handler, ask a different question: *will that infected Mimic still be on those stairs when I need them clear?* A spit followed by a cloud should permit an immediate rupture in an ordinary encounter. Keeping infection working in a patch could instead defend the stairs while the operator searches. Don't import elemental recipe charts: a player should be able to predict the result from the host and the patch they see. Under [37](37-lurkers-among-civilians.md), clouds, chains and devices leave human-looking figures untouched. Neither watching a shard nor laying a patch can expose a lurker; a damage reveal needs a deliberate aimed finishing hit. The rig remains a separate, mutual way to reveal.

### What can a variant change without taking over the kit?

[Path of Exile's skill-gem overview](https://www.pathofexile.com/game) uses Fireball to explain supports: the same skill can chain to another enemy or split into multiple projectiles. Compare [Roboquest's class description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/692890/Roboquest/): drone command and attacking from stealth are examples of different class identities, each with its own upgrade choices. The former reference helps change an action; the latter helps decide when a change would amount to replacing the operator. The store description does not establish particular perk interactions.

**Proposal.** Keep the Rifleman's marked-target area burst. In a waiting room, aim the spending shot at the revealed Mimic surrounded by others. In a long passage, a Barracks alternative could send that same discharge along the line beyond the mark. Now the operator rolls for a different angle and loses coverage beside the target. No auto-aim on basics, no extra shot plus the old splash, and no socket hunt before the first marked shot works. This gives [39](39-class-levels-and-barracks-variants.md) two rooms in which to explain the choice. [27](27-skill-tree-reference-brushwild.md) is useful for making a swap legible and reversible; its trial tree is not the final tree.

### Does a find change this raid, or just raise a number?

Three references separate decisions that are easy to collapse into one green upgrade arrow:

| Checked example | Useful distinction for DarkSignal | What to leave behind, and why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In *Hades*, [boons enhance abilities](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1145360/Hades/). The [Thunder Signet keepsake](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/hades-keepsakes-tier-list) steers a coming boon toward Zeus; it doesn't specify which blessing to take. | Giving a player a reason to search a field workshop for hardware is different from putting a sentry upgrade straight into their hands. The search still has a risk and an uncertain outcome. | Don't promise a particular build at deployment. Needing a lucky offer to call the first sentry shot would hollow out the base kit. |
| Blizzard's [Diablo IV design preview](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23583664/diablo-iv-quarterly-update-december-2020) illustrated skill damage with Strength for a Barbarian and Intelligence for a Sorceress. Its later [Loot Reborn article](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24077223/galvanize-your-legend-in-season-4-loot-reborn) separately described salvaging Legendary Aspects into a reusable Codex for imprinting. These are different snapshots, not a current formula. | In a comparison, show what makes a hit stronger separately from the rule that changes what the hit does. A wider field of fire matters in a different room from a harder shot. | Don't turn the tree into a main-stat purchase, or let field salvage instantly bank a power. Appraisal and the trip home give a find its value in [08](08-loot-contamination-and-legendary-gear.md), [23](23-demo-boss-loot-and-drops.md) and [26](26-equipment-loot-reference-brushwild.md). |
| In Housemarque's [Returnal survival guide](https://blog.playstation.com/2021/04/28/returnal-survival-guide-housemarques-gameplay-tips-for-atropos/), a Parasite has a benefit and a drawback. Adrenaline offers another kind of wager: taking damage resets its accumulated benefits. | A field modification can be worth accepting on the way in and worth passing on the way out. Make both effects readable before the choice when the modification is offered openly. | Don't let one hit strip the whole kit of its identity, or import Adrenaline's through-wall vision. The operator still has a complete kit after a mistake, and sensing still exposes the operator. |

**First question for a later playtest:** In an ordinary fight before finding any item, can the player point to the target, doorway or firing position that made them spend now? Ask again on the return trip with intel in the bag. If both answers are just “M2 came off cooldown,” try [28's](28-class-resource-loops.md) smaller routine Charge gain before adding more sources. If the first answer is “I was waiting for a kill,” fix access to the signature before offering variants. These are questions for play, not reported results.
