# Staged Damage Calculation

Status: Design research and proposal. Companion to [Skill Resource and Synergies](24-skill-resource-and-synergies.md) and [Class Resource Loops](28-class-resource-loops.md). [Back to index](README.md)

A marked Mimic bursts in a doorway. The gunner can explain the result: “I chose the creature with a pack behind it.” If a rifle affix strengthens the shot, the burst inherits the stronger shot, and the same affix strengthens the burst again, the best choice soon becomes collecting copies of the affix. The mark and the doorway hardly matter. Staging is a way to preserve the value of that shot without letting a bonus change jobs halfway through it.

## What the demo does now

In `src/sim/loot.js`, `gearStats` already gathers like affixes from equipped pieces. `Raid.recalc` in `src/sim/raid.js` combines gear, raid growth and run items; some contributions already add within a family, while others independently scale the attack. `compileSkills` in `src/sim/skill-build.js` draws on `ITEMS` in `src/sim/data.js` and `SKILL_KITS` in `src/sim/skill-data.js` for conditional bonuses, slot behavior and triggers. An item description alone doesn't establish what a hit does.

`Raid.playerHit` distinguishes melee, skills, summons and ongoing harm. Eligible hits can crit; ongoing harm cannot. `Raid.damageEnemy` then considers the Matron's state, target vulnerability, Brands and breakable plating before health is lost. Incoming attacks take a different path: `Raid.damagePlayer` checks dodge protection, then damage reduction and barrier absorption. These are concrete defenses; they don't establish a general elemental resistance table.

Timing already matters. `piercingLine` in `src/sim/skills.js` remembers Suppression for the shot but clears it before the target-side Brand check. `whirlSlash` hits before spending Exposed. `Raid.consume` can refund Charge and cause a separate item hit; `castSpender` also allows limited copies, which cannot start another round of copies. The risk is that a future affix might change all those outcomes by accident. This is a source reading, not a finding that every follow-up currently double-dips.

## What the references establish

For **Diablo IV**, [Maxroll's damage guide](https://maxroll.gg/d4/getting-started/damage-for-beginners) describes weapon damage and the skill as the starting point for a typical weapon hit. Applicable additive affixes gather into a common bonus, even when their labels differ. That combined contribution and explicitly independent multipliers scale the base. A qualifying critical strike or a Vulnerable target has a distinct baseline multiplier; ordinary *extra* critical or Vulnerable damage on gear joins the additive pool only when its condition is met. A critical hit does not turn every critical affix into a fresh multiplier. On the receiving side, [the in-depth guide’s discussion of enemy damage reduction](https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/in-depth-damage-guide) distinguishes what the attacker produces from what the target actually loses. This is an order for understanding a hit, from source to health lost, not an official engine trace or a claim about every Diablo release.

John identifies Diablo as an influence on StormBound. The official StormBound RESULT_CARD's *Resolution, chains and damage attribution* gives that comparison substance: its historical combat model separates the action's base, additive bonuses, independent multipliers, crit and the recipient's condition; it also distinguishes run items and mode effects. The browser evidence is narrower. A delayed or shared attack retains its source, and each actual recipient gets its own defense. In *Stacking, refreshing, exclusivity and elemental reactions*, spending an infection can use harm still to come; refreshing it doesn't earn harm for time already passed. *Interactions with items, units, bosses and modes* even distinguishes a turret's inherited attack from another direct hit by its owner. The card describes an older hit order and later corrections, not a verified universal browser formula or a complete resistance model. Its separate untracked draft is not evidence here.

## Proposal: give every contribution a home

Use a consistent order so a new item cannot change the result just by being equipped last. Let the action establish what was committed: a rifle shot, a sentry order, the remaining sting of an infection. Gather bonuses that strengthen the same part of that action together, whether they came from a run item or a piece of gear. Reserve independent changes for decisions the operator can recognize, such as spending a Brand. Let the recipient explain the rest: the round rang against a facing plate, or found the gap at the side. A sufficiently powerful shot can still punch through the front and cut a path through the horde. Flanking saves the heavy shot for something else.

Treat a Brand as a choice with a consequence. When a mark is spent to catch a pack, a nearby target gets to answer the burst with its own defenses; it doesn't inherit the first victim's stagger. Today's Overdrive changes when the spender is affordable. Tomorrow's class reserve could change where the discharge lands. Neither needs a silent increase to every later hit. A branch that moves the burst along the firing line changes the shot to look for; keeping both the line and the old splash at full strength would quietly turn the tree into a power ladder.

Use the same distinction outside gunfire. A parachute changes the landing impact, not the operator's rifle damage. Venom that makes a fresh wound more dangerous has a different job from venom already draining health. Repeated exposure may be frightening without paying for the same wound again. Avoid using final health loss as the starting strength of a second attack, and avoid granting every new affix a multiplier of its own.

**Later design order:** First clarify what a plain hit carries and which similar bonuses already belong together. Compare a marked shot with a shot against the same plate from the flank. Once those differences are intelligible, follow a delayed burst or poison tick back to its source. That gives future gear and tree variants a familiar hit to change, and leaves open which exceptions actually earn their place.
