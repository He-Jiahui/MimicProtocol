# Class Levels and Barracks Variants

Status: Design proposal. Extends [Meta Progression](07-meta-progression-and-difficulty.md), [Demo Class Kits](18-demo-classes-elites-and-items.md) and [Class Resource Loops](28-class-resource-loops.md). [Back to index](README.md)

After a raid, the most interesting question in the Barracks might be “where did you wish that burst had landed?” Give persistent class level a purpose in answering it: experience with the kit opens alternative ways to use it on the next deployment. Shared Operator Rank can recognize service across classes; raid finds still give this particular outing its shape. A class returning with an empty bag should still have a complete signature to play. The [Brushwild tree study](27-skill-tree-reference-brushwild.md) is useful for showing a choice and a way back. Its example tree is not the final DarkSignal tree.

**A tree changes mechanisms.** The native M1, M2, S and R kit is the subject of the tree. A branch should alter where a player aims, where a resource waits, or which opportunity they let pass. A higher class level needn't add a damage purchase between interesting branches. In the Barracks, an example of a room where the new choice helps, beside one where the original choice is easier, says more than an upward arrow. Let a returning player try the old branch again. A variant is the chosen side of an exclusive fork, bought with the class points of [27](27-skill-tree-reference-brushwild.md) and swapped free in the Barracks; doc 18's replacement M2-B and R-B behaviors (`PLANNED_VARIANTS`) stay a separate selection.

| Operator | A branch and the decision it changes |
| --- | --- |
| Rifleman | Keep the gunner's marked-target area burst (proposed in [28](28-class-resource-loops.md); today Piercing Line only pierces and ricochets) available in ordinary play. A variant could send its discharge down the chosen firing lane instead of around the marked Mimic. The rifle still paints Suppression and the player still aims the spending shot; the variant gives a reason to roll to the end of the corridor rather than fire from the doorway. |
| Blade | A guard fragment shaken loose by an aimed cut might protect the operator who lunges in to continue the exchange. A retreating variant could instead catch the fragment on the way back from a whirl. Both can pay off against a living enemy. Waiting for a kill, or for a move that already needs a refund, shouldn't be the class's ordinary reward. |
| Summoner | Keep Mark as a call to field hardware. An emplacement variant might hold the order for a target crossing its firing lane; a mobile variant could carry the order out with the drones. The first makes a defended relay a good post, the other makes sense on an escort. Leaving a sentry behind should mean giving up that position, not gaining its effect on every drone as well. |
| Toxic Handler | A spit and a cloud should be enough to see an ordinary rupture. A cultivating variant could leave infection on its host as it moves through a pool; the alternative spends it now to clear the stairhead. The real choice is whether to keep that patch of ground. The player shouldn't need a rare drop or an unusually durable victim to find out what the kit does. |

The shared Charge bar already feels full too often. Extra storage and automatic refunds as compulsory tree purchases would hide the problem. Give the base classes reasons to spend, then let variants reshape those reasons. [Staged Damage Calculation](30-staged-damage-calculation.md) supplies the boundary: bending the gunner's discharge into a line moves the payoff; it doesn't also apply an independent damage bonus to the mark, the round and the burst. A veteran may make a better shot because they know the route and chose the right variant.

**Room for further class identities.** A future rescue specialist might build cover by holding a position under pressure, then choose between carrying that protection on an escort and leaving it at the pickup. A salvage specialist might recover spent field components with a deliberately aimed tether, choosing between packing them for the next room and committing them to a device here. These belong in consideration as separate kits only if their ordinary decisions feel unlike Blade's momentum or the Summoner's firing orders. They are possibilities beyond the current roster, not identities unlocked by turning an existing class into someone else.
