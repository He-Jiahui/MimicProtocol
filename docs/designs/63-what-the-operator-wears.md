# What the Operator Wears

Status: Design direction with proposed affixes, wear and powers. Updated: 2026-11-27. Requirements: R-01, R-05, R-12, D-20, D-22.

Revises [08: Equipment and Power Cores](08-loot-contamination-and-legendary-gear.md), [23: Boss drops](23-demo-boss-loot-and-drops.md) and [26: Equipment comparison](26-equipment-loot-reference-brushwild.md). Extends [38: Stash appraisal](38-field-bags-and-stash-appraisal.md), [52: Barracks attributes](52-barracks-attributes-and-raid-stats.md), [57: Ownership](57-what-crosses-extraction.md) and [59: Raid items](59-what-a-raid-item-can-do.md).

**The operator wears the kit chosen at the hub for the whole deployment.** That kit is kept on death, with its accumulated wear. A piece found in the district, from a plain drawer or from a boss, cannot be worn there. Carry it through extraction or recover it with the [drone](62-what-the-insurance-drone-brings-home.md); either route delivers unidentified cargo. An active [backpack](61-packing-for-the-walk-out.md) holds cargo and drops on death. Neither bag nor drone is worn equipment. A doc 59 universal pickup can change the fight immediately and expires at either ending; it produces no stash copy, donor core or training point.

## From ferry dispatch to the Lab

At the drawer the player sees **sealed equipment, Prototype quality**. Its footprint and weight answer whether it fits and how it will carry. Those are handling facts; quality is the only equipment property disclosed. Identical packaging can contain different slots. No name, slot preview, Prime sparkle, affix, power outline, comparison arrow or sale estimate derived from the hidden roll. Tainted cargo can require handling precautions without advertising its identity. Crossing the gate changes who owns the parcel, not what the operator knows about it.

The hub stash gives two honest choices. Sell sealed for a bid based on visible quality alone, or spend Lab Samples to learn what's inside. Two equally graded parcels get the same unopened bid, even when only one has a power. Use that public bid for the sealed piece in [19’s carried-value display](19-demo-core-loop-and-progression.md) and extraction bonus too, or a player could weigh the hidden roll by watching the payout change. **Appraisal reveals the existing piece; it does not reroll it.** Full design: appraise at the Containment Lab, then decontaminate if needed before use. Demo direction: keep the existing combined **Process** action. On return to the stash, keep the piece selected. Suppose it opens into headgear that holds a trace through broken sight. Show what the fitted headpiece would give up, the new rule and its cost on the same class with no raid buffs. A useful piece may command a good identified price and still be wrong for this operator. This extends [38](38-field-bags-and-stash-appraisal.md); it proposes a sealed sale where today's `sellItem` requires processing first.

## Five places on the body

Primary weapon and sidearm remain separate weapon decisions in the full design. The demo has one weapon and no sidearm button, so it wears six pieces: the weapon and the five below. The five slots below describe clothing and protection; they do not replace the weapons or the four native class skills. The sense rig is still operator equipment in the fiction. Its wearable scan modifiers now live on the headpiece. Arms and legs do not survive as extra slots. The second column gives examples of **exclusive lines**: a generic attribute on another slot need not reproduce their specific rule.

| Worn slot | What to look for after appraisal |
| --- | --- |
| Head | Sensor headgear: a readable trace from an already completed scan lasts longer. No free reveal; initiating the scan still exposes the operator. |
| Body | Armor: impact protection on the covered facing. High frontal reduction still lets damage through, and leaves the flank worth defending. |
| Hands | Weapon handling: a braced grip adds damage to a deliberate weapon hit after the operator steadies. It neither aims the shot nor boosts every tick of a poison pool. |
| Waist | Medical webbing: a readied medkit takes less time to apply. It changes exposure while treating a wound, without creating another kit or shielding one on death. |
| Shoes | Footwear: loaded walking pace, or a choice about where a dash ends. Extra displacement need not also buy protection, more charges or a shorter cooldown. |

Generic advanced-attribute affixes can appear on **any** body slot. Proposal: an identified waist piece can add a modest fitted Conditioning allowance instead of a medical line. Show trained Conditioning and the equipped contribution separately in the hub preview. Apply the allowance through [52's conversion](52-barracks-attributes-and-raid-stats.md) to the starting direct sheet once; removing the piece removes its contribution, leaving training untouched. Don't then count the same bonus again as a direct movement affix. Finding that piece in the street, carrying it, rescuing it or merely identifying it grants **zero** aptitude. Raid items can change direct stats for that outing only. Compare like bonuses under [53](53-calculating-raid-stats-and-damage.md) and budget the whole group of benefits under [54](54-keeping-attribute-growth-stable.md).

## Keep the piece, pay for using it

Proposal: condition follows the deployed piece across raids. A vest that absorbs hits comes back more worn than one carried through a quiet exit. An operator who dies after that same exchange keeps the same vest with that wear; insurance is irrelevant. Show condition and its concrete consequence at debrief and before deployment. A service-due vest might give reduced protection on the next outing; repair at the Armory restores serviceability without improving the roll. A fully worn piece can stay owned but need repair before deployment. Stashed finds don't wear out on the shelf. Prefer predictable service and an available basic kit over random jams, surprise erasure of a signature, or a mandatory payment after every short outing. No repair price, wear rate or durability breakpoint is settled here; [64](64-implementation-plan.md) records the starting values used in the build.

## Who owns the exceptional rule?

An affix can shorten a scan or alter protection. A high-quality piece can also change a **decision**. Example proposal for headgear: after a mutual scan, keep the *last known position* of one revealed contact around a corner, at the cost of delaying the next pulse. The trace stays where contact was lost; it does not follow a target through walls. The normal scan works without the piece. Equip the piece for a cautious retreat; take it off for frequent scans. That is a comparison worth showing even when the ratings tie.

- **Fixed on the piece.** The demo's slot Aspect in [24](24-skill-resource-and-synergies.md) and [23's proposed fixed Legendary Latent Power](23-demo-boss-loot-and-drops.md) are distinct. Reveal them at Process and describe each separately when both exist. A distinctive Unique, if ever pursued, keeps its defining power and drawback on that particular item. No new rarity is required here.
- **Moved at the Lab, in the full design only.** Keep [08's](08-loot-contamination-and-legendary-gear.md) consume-the-donor Power Core route for *transferable* powers: appraise, clean if needed, sacrifice the donor, fit the one core to a compatible host at the hub. A sensor power fits appropriate headgear, not every body part. Once installed, only the fitted piece supplies it. No second copy on the donor, no account-wide imprint, no extraction of a fixed Unique signature. The demo's fixed Latent Power does not imply core transfer is implemented.
- **Never acquired by the raid pool.** A doc 59 coil can arc from a deliberate hit to a revealed hostile for this raid. It never turns into the sensor rule, levels it, or unlocks a class variant. Conversely, neither headgear nor a core needs coil stacks to work. Keep [58's variants](58-choosing-a-class-variant.md) about ways to play the same native skills: the Rifleman's aimed mark-for-area payoff still works in issue gear. Avoid generic Charge refill on equipment when the bank is already often full [under 28](28-class-resource-loops.md). No follow-up supplies auto-aim or lets area effects test human-looking people for a disguise.
